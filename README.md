Hi, I'm Andrea 👋 I'm a postdoctoral researcher at [Inria](https://www.inria.fr/), in the [Wimmics](https://team.inria.fr/wimmics/) team at [Université Côte d'Azur](https://univ-cotedazur.fr/), and I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Mathematics from the [University of Bari Aldo Moro](https://www.uniba.it/).

I study how people and AI systems can work *together*: I'm interested in AI that augments human abilities rather than replacing them, and that people can understand, question, and trust. These days I'm building LLM-based conversational assistants that help scientists explore metabolomics knowledge graphs.

**Research interests:** Human-Centered & Symbiotic AI · Human–AI Interaction · Explainable AI · Conversational AI & Knowledge Graphs · Usability, UX & Accessibility

As a strong supporter of Free (as in "libre") Software, here you'll find my research code, side projects, and configuration files.
